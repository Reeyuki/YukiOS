YukiOS Remote

Enter the 6-character code shown on your YukiOS desktop to connect and control your PC remotely.

Open Yuki Remote Desktop on your PC (get it from GitHub Releases), click Share Desktop, then enter the 6-character code above and click Connect. Your mouse and keyboard control the remote PC in real time. Press Esc to disconnect. The connection is end-to-end encrypted via WebRTC.
